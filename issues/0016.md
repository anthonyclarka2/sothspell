"A viewer needs to take the board out of the app. PNG and JPG should capture the
current view or the whole board. PDF should use a print-specific reading layout
with selectable text, not a screenshot pasted into a PDF. Exports must hide
controls, toasts, and selection, and must restore the board afterwards."
