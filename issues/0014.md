# "Canvas: high contrast, colour-blind palette, and 200% text"

"The canvas must keep its meaning for low-vision and colour-blind viewers:
system high contrast, increased contrast, a colour-blind-safe palette, and text
up to 200% without clipping or lost actions. Colour must never be the only
signal for a relation, state, or focus."
