# "Canvas: add zoom, fit, home, auto-arrange, and fullscreen controls"

"The canvas needs a visible control surface for navigation: zoom in/out, reset,
fit all, home, auto-arrange, fullscreen, and outline. Fullscreen needs a shortcut,
a brief button highlight, and a message that tells the viewer how to return. The
brief leaves the shortcut undefined; choose one and document it."
